To add your profile photo:

1. Name your photo file exactly: profile-photo.jpg
2. Drag it into this folder (/public).
3. Refresh the site — it'll show automatically in the circular photo
   slot on the homepage.

Until you add one, the site shows your initials instead, so nothing
looks broken.

(If your photo is a .png or .jpeg, either rename it to
profile-photo.jpg, or open components/ProfilePhoto.tsx and change
the src="/profile-photo.jpg" line to match your file's name.)
